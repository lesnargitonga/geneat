# LESNAR Motion System v0.1

Issued by the owner, 30 September 2026. Governing doctrine for all LESNAR
motion work. Recorded here as the source of truth; not to be reinterpreted.

## Core principle

Generated motion may reveal something that exists.
It may not manufacture evidence that does not.

Every LESNAR film operates across three layers.

### 1. Truth

Real systems. Real interfaces, real telemetry, real maps, real transactions,
real environments, real captured behavior and real measured data.

Truth is never regenerated merely to make it prettier.

### 2. Spatial interpretation

The invisible system becomes physical. Architecture may separate into layers.
Data may acquire direction and mass. Relationships may become visible. Cameras
may travel through spaces that cannot exist physically.

This is where Higgsfield, 3D and cinematic generation operate.

Spatial interpretation may explain truth. It may not replace truth.

### 3. Editorial precision

Typography, labels, diagrams, numerical values, logos, disclosure language,
interface crops and final timing remain deterministic.

AI video is not trusted with exact text, exact UI or exact numerical data.

## Camera grammar

**Reality → impossible access → system logic → consequence → reality**

Begin somewhere concrete. The camera then crosses a boundary a physical camera
could not cross. Inside, the system becomes understandable. The sequence ends by
reconnecting the abstraction to the real system.

Camera motion should have purpose. No orbit merely because an orbit looks
expensive. No constant parallax. No arbitrary speed ramps. No generic
drone-through-circuit-board shots.

## Material grammar

No universal cyber material. System materials follow function.

Operational infrastructure should feel dense, physical and engineered. Trust
boundaries should feel separated. Evidence should feel persistent and
inspectable. Intelligence may reveal hidden relationships but should not become
a glowing magic cloud. Real product UI remains sharp and planar rather than
being regenerated as fictional holograms.

## Data motion

Data is not decorative particle noise. Every visible signal should answer at
least one question: where did it originate, where is it going, what transformed
it, what relationship does it represent, why is the viewer seeing it.

Events may branch. Evidence may consolidate. Telemetry may stream. Identity may
bind. Anomalies may diverge. Relationships may reconstruct.

Random glowing dots are not LESNAR motion language.

## Typography

Editorial, not generated. Text appears because the viewer needs orientation.
Avoid paragraphs floating in cinematic space.

Prefer one system name, one state, one exact value, one consequential phrase.

Typography should remain stable while the world around it moves.

## Sound

Sound follows system state rather than trailer convention. Low mechanical
presence, restrained data transients, spatial movement cues, silence before
consequential transitions, physical landing / contact moments where real source
permits.

Avoid generic cinematic booms every three seconds.
