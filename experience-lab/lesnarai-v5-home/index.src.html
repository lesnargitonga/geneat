Live Home healthcare

CarePro

Home nurses a family can trust.

A platform that matches families with nurses for care at home, with payment held by CarePro until the care is delivered. No nurse is offered to a family until they have passed four checks:

  1. Credentials. ID, nursing licence and certificate of good conduct, checked by hand.
  2. Registry check. Licence confirmed with the Nursing Council of Kenya, and its expiry tracked.
  3. Interview. Structured, and scored against a clinical rubric.
  4. Clinical sign-off. A registered nurse reviews the whole file and decides.

The site publishes its own count. On 16 September 2026, 20 nurses had joined and none had yet been approved for assignments.

MediMatch

Moving surplus medicine to the hospitals that need it.

One hospital has medicine about to expire on the shelf while another has run out. MediMatch finds the match and plans the transfer, ranked by distance, urgency, verification and product fit. It grew out of field research on Nairobi’s public health facilities at USIU-Africa.

Research Drone autonomy

Operation Sentinel

A drone that flies its mission on its own.

I built the software between a drone’s autopilot and its mission. It takes a route, flies it with nobody at the controls, and records 59 measurements about five times a second: position, speed, battery, wind and what its sensors see. Every flight is sealed with a SHA-256 manifest, so its record cannot be quietly edited afterwards.

Research Trading research

Gold Trader

A trading assistant that tells me when not to trade.

I built a research system for trading gold, and made it audit itself before any real money moves. The audit was brutal: taking every signal for 30 days would have lost −$79,297 over 4,738 trades. So five of the nine strategies were cut, and the rest stay on paper.

What held up: one narrow setup, up +2.65R over seven trades, which is too few to trust. Paper yes, live no, until twenty forward trades confirm it.

Paper trading only. Live orders are locked. Figures from the audit of 29 April to 29 May 2026.

In development Private messaging

Simy

A messenger its own server can’t read.

Messages are locked on your phone before they leave it, and unlocked only on the other person’s. The server in between carries sealed envelopes it has no way to open. The encryption core (X3DH and Double Ratchet) and the relay are built and tested in Rust.

Not yet a finished messenger, and not independently audited.

Model Foundry

How I decide which AI to trust.

Before an AI model touches real work, I test it on my own machine. One test: the same coding model run two ways. One task hid a sneaky instruction inside the work: llama.cpp ignored it, and Ollama obeyed it, both times.

Share of tasks passed

llama.cpp80%
Ollama60%

Qwen2.5-Coder-7B-Instruct, Q4_K_M, pinned by SHA-256. Ten tasks, two repetitions, one machine.

Verdict: fine for coding help with a person reading the output. Not for accepting code on its own, security decisions, or enforcing instruction boundaries. No fine-tuned model has been produced yet.
