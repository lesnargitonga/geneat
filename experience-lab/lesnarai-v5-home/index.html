MediMatch

Supply expires in one hospital while another county runs out.

MediMatch finds surplus medical stock and routes it to the facilities short of it, ranked by distance, urgency, verification and product fit. It grew out of field research on Nairobi County’s health facilities at USIU-Africa, and runs as a national command floor on a real map of Kenya.

One run of the demonstration, over synthetic inventory. The longest route it ranked sends 120 units of insulin from Kenyatta National Hospital to Mandera. No facility supplied stock and no transfer was executed.

Operation Sentinel

A drone may appear on the operator’s screen only if the simulator confirms it exists.

A command-and-control stack for autonomous UAV simulation. It is built to fly a PX4 x500 in Gazebo Harmonic through a MAVSDK bridge that records every sample, and its backend registers an aircraft only when real telemetry arrives. Each run is sealed with a SHA-256 manifest of everything it wrote.

LESNAR AI built the autonomy bridge, mission logic, telemetry pipeline and operator system, and integrated PX4, Gazebo and AirSim, which are the work of their own upstream maintainers.

Simy

The server that carries a message must not be able to read it.

A Rust workspace with a shared cryptographic core for X3DH and Double Ratchet, and a relay on PostgreSQL and Redis that stores only encrypted envelopes, rejects replays and expires what it holds. The boundary below is the design.

Sender’s device

  • Identity keys
  • Session and ratchet state
  • The message itself

Relay

Holds

  • Mailbox ID
  • A hash of the retrieval secret
  • Public prekey bundles and device records
  • Replay token
  • The encrypted envelope
  • Expiry time

Never holds

  • Message text
  • Private keys
  • Session secrets and ratchet state

Recipient’s device

  • Identity keys
  • Session and ratchet state
  • The message, once opened

CarePro

A nurse is assigned to a family only after four published checks.

  1. Credentials. ID, nursing licence and certificate of good conduct, reviewed by hand.
  2. Registry check. Licence verified against the Nursing Council of Kenya, expiry tracked.
  3. Interview. Structured and scored against a clinical rubric.
  4. Clinical sign-off. A registered nurse reviews the whole file and decides.

Gold Trader

A trading signal is not an edge until an audit says so.

An autonomous XAUUSD research system with a supervised paper-trading loop. Its thirty-day audit ran nine strategy families through the same engine the live scout uses. Most failed, and were cut.

Model Foundry

A model earns its place, or loses it.

Where models are evaluated locally before they are trusted with any work. No fine-tuned model has been produced yet. One evaluation, start to finish:

Model
Qwen2.5-Coder-7B-Instruct, Q4_K_M, pinned by SHA-256 before testing
Runtimes
llama.cpp and Ollama, same weights, same machine
Result
llama.cpp passed 80% of tasks, 8 of 10 repeatable. Ollama passed 60%, 6 of 10.
Failure
One task hid an instruction inside the work. llama.cpp ignored it. Ollama obeyed it, in both repetitions.
Verdict
Fit for local coding work with a person reading the output. Not for accepting code on its own, security decisions, or enforcing instruction boundaries.

Ten tasks, two repetitions, one machine. Not a benchmark.
