Live product · carepro.co.ke

CarePro

CarePro publishes four verification steps. This is that sequence as the product states it — described by the product, not measured by us.

  1. 01Credentials National ID, nursing council licence and certificate of good conduct. Uploaded certificates are reviewed by hand.
  2. 02Registry check Licence verified against the Nursing Council of Kenya register, with expiry tracked continuously.
  3. 03Interview A structured interview, scored on a clinical rubric.
  4. 04Clinical sign-off A registered nurse reviews the whole file and approves, or doesn’t.

How the work is done

Built here, then operated here.

We do not hand over a repository and leave. Products run as separate services, deployed and kept running, with the operator screens the staff actually work in.

What we build

  • Whole productsCustomer surface, backend, database, deployment, and the operator screens behind them.
  • Payments and messagingM-Pesa STK push and card payment, ordering over WhatsApp, and reconciling a provider event against the internal record.
  • AI you can inspectRetrieval and tool-calling held as structure in the repository, with the decision boundary written down rather than buried in a prompt.

How we keep it running

  • InfrastructureSeparate runtimes with cross-database denial and isolated cache namespaces.
  • Security and hardeningPort scanning, service banner collection, log watching with a sliding-window detector, and host firewall state.
  • Embedded and physicalControl that keeps running on a small board with no network to fall back on. Greenhouse control is owner-attested, not measured here.
