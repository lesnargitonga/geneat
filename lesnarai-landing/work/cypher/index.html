The system that must ask permission

  1. Tenant checkEvery retrieval needs a validated tenant. Without one, it fails closed.
  2. RetrievalEvidence comes only from that tenant; crossing tenants needs an explicit admin mode.
  3. GuardrailsPrompt-injection and unsupported-claim checks are pinned as tests.
  4. PolicyPolicy and SHIELD runtimes decide what may be proposed.
  5. A personAn operator approves. Live sending is gated and not enabled.
