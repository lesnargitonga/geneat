Every system, by stage

Live2 · people use it now

  • CarePro Book a vetted nurse to care for someone at home, with payment arranged through the platform. A controlled public beta. HealthRecord
  • BizMtaani A neighbourhood marketplace: shops list what they sell, customers order and pay from a phone. One merchant is trading. CommerceRecord

Prototype7 · it works; nobody depends on it yet

  • Operation Sentinel An autonomy stack that flies a PX4 aircraft through a mission. Proven in simulation; no physical flight. AutonomyCase study
  • Zygos Before you pay a business you found online, it shows who is behind it and what is known, weighing independent evidence above the business's own word. Trust and safetyCase study in preparation
  • MediMatch Makes surplus and shortage of medical supplies visible across facilities and routes transfers over real roads. Research prototype on synthetic inventory. HealthCase study in preparation
  • Jamii Projects Hub Plan community projects, vote on what matters and keep clear records for a family, chama or welfare group. Deployed; a pilot is being prepared. CommunityRecord
  • Gen-Eat Order from a campus café over WhatsApp and collect it when it is ready. A demonstration; no café is live on it yet. CommerceRecord
  • Hazina Nomads Kenyan gifts and heritage pieces, sourced for travellers, diaspora families and companies. The storefront is online; ordering is paused. CommerceRecord
  • LESNAR PLAY Couch co-op on a television: the TV hosts the game and phones join as controllers by scanning a code. DevicesNo public page yet

Internal2 · we run it on ourselves

  • LESNAR TV A fast, ad-free home screen for a 2 GB television, in daily use on our own. An alpha, measured on the device. DevicesNo public page yet
  • SentinelCore The evidence, event and identity foundation for our security work. Its proof and qualification layer, Sentinel Assurance, runs authorised assessment, retest and closure. SecurityRecord

Research2 · nothing operated

  • Cypher Rules about what an AI system is allowed to do, and a person who has to approve it before anything happens. AI systemsRecord
  • Gold Trader Research into automated gold trading, tested only on paper. It places no real orders with anyone's money. Finance researchRecord

A case study exists where the evidence is deep enough to prove the work; other systems have a shorter record, or none yet. Some of our work belongs to clients and appears here only with their permission.
