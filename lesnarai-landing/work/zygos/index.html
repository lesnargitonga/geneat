Zygos

Before you pay a business you found online, Zygos shows who is behind it and what is known about it. It never calls anyone a scammer. It shows the evidence and where it came from.

Demonstrated · pre-productionprivate sourcedemonstration records are fictional

Four rules the system will not break

  1. A claim is not a factWhat a reporter says happened is recorded as their claim, kept apart from the evidence about it.
  2. A business's own word is not proofWhat a business declares about itself is evidence to weigh, never a verification.
  3. Authority is not independenceDifferent web addresses or authors never count as independent sources.
  4. Every decision leaves a trailReports, responses and moderation decisions are written to an audit chain.
