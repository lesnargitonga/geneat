LESNAR PLAY

Couch co-op without a console. The television runs the game and its own local relay, and phones join as controllers by scanning a code.

Prototypegame: Comet Circuitno app · no account · LAN only

The phone is the controller

No console, no laptop, no cloud

  1. The TV hostsThe game and a WebSocket relay run on the television itself.
  2. A code to joinThe screen shows a QR code and a four-digit join code, rate-limited.
  3. The phone playsThe pad opens in the phone's browser. Nothing to install, no account.
  4. Nothing leaves the roomInputs travel over the local network only, under a strict content policy.
