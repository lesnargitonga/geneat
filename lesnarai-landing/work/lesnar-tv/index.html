What it is

  1. Learns, locallyOrders your apps by how you use them, on the television, with no account.
  2. Four themesGlass, Ink, Classic and High Contrast.
  3. SpacesGroups apps by what you are doing: watch, listen, play.
  4. LinkA phone companion paired by QR code, usable as a remote.

What this page does not show

  • Distribution: it is not published, and there is no release signing key yet.
  • Meeting its budgets: navigation smoothness and Glass memory are over target.
  • A screensaver: one was pulled after it stopped responding to keys.
  • Real apps in these frames: the screens are emulator captures with fictional apps.
