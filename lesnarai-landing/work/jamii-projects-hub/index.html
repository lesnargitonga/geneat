A paper trail
for every shilling

Organising usually lives in notebooks and WhatsApp. Jamii gives each step a record, and lets people take part without a smartphone.

  1. IdeaA project is submitted, by phone, on paper or through a community champion.
  2. VoteMembers vote; verified votes are required for decisions.
  3. FundingContributions are recorded against the project, never as loans or donations.
  4. ExecutionProgress is tracked in the open.
  5. ReportThe outcome stays on the record.
