CarePro

Home nursing for families in Nairobi. A family is matched to a verified nurse, and payment is arranged through the platform.

Controlled public betaOffline when last checked, 2 Nov 2026LESNAR AI engineered the platformno claim about who owns or runs the service

The gate before
a nurse is sent

No nurse reaches a family until every check has passed, and the last check is a registered nurse reading the whole file.

  1. DocumentsThe applicant submits identity and professional documents.
  2. LicenceThe licence is checked against the Nursing Council register.
  3. InterviewA structured interview is scored against a clinical rubric.
  4. Clinical reviewA registered nurse reviews the whole file and signs off.
  5. ApprovalOnly then can the nurse be offered work.

What the public sees

After the gate

  1. A redacted offerThe nurse sees the visit without the patient's details.
  2. AcceptancePatient details are revealed only after the nurse accepts.
  3. PaymentPayment is confirmed through the platform before the visit.
  4. Staff accessEvery staff account signs in with two factors; an audit browser records what was done.

What this page does not show

  • Any patient, nurse or booking screen: only public pages are shown.
  • Automated refunds or payout runs: not built.
  • Ownership of the service: this page describes our engineering only.
