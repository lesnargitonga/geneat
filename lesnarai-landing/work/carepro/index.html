Work / Health

CarePro

Home nursing for families in Nairobi. A family is matched to a verified nurse, and payment is arranged through the platform.

Controlled public betaLESNAR AI engineered the platformno claim about who owns or runs the service

The gate before
a nurse is sent

A family is letting a stranger into their home to care for someone vulnerable. CarePro's job is to make that trust earned: no nurse reaches a family until every check has passed, and the last check is a registered nurse reading the whole file.

  1. DocumentsThe applicant submits identity and professional documents.
  2. LicenceThe licence is checked against the Nursing Council register.
  3. InterviewA structured interview is scored against a clinical rubric.
  4. Clinical reviewA registered nurse reviews the whole file and signs off.
  5. ApprovalOnly then can the nurse be offered work.

What the public sees

From request to visit

  1. RequestCare at home, while travelling, at an event or for a company, with any equipment and a preferred nurse nearby.
  2. MatchStaff assign a ranked candidate, or the best match in one step. Only an approved nurse with a current licence can be assigned.
  3. A redacted offerThe nurse sees the visit without the patient's details until accepting; every read of medical details is logged.
  4. Payment heldPaid by M-Pesa in the app and held until care is delivered; never cash to a caregiver.
  5. The visitCheck-in and check-out by location, with care notes.
  6. AfterwardsA review, an updated nurse score, and earnings accrued.

How it is built

  1. Documents kept privateChecked by content, not name; stored privately; opened only through short-lived, logged links.
  2. AccountsRevocable sessions, mandatory two-factor sign-in for staff, and lockout after repeated failures.
  3. RequestsForgery checks on every change; rate limits kept in the database so they survive restarts.
  4. MoneyWhole minor units, with fee plus nurse payout always equal to the booking total.
  5. AutomationEvery 30 minutes: notices by email, SMS and WhatsApp, unpaid bookings cancelled, unanswered offers released, expiring licences warned, then suspended.
  6. AuditAn append-only trail of staff actions, with its own browser.
