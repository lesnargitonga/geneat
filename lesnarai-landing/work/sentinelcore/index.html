The assurance loop

Sentinel Assurance: every step is authorised, recorded and retested before anything is closed.

  1. AssetA tenant asset with a stable, canonical identity.
  2. Policy and authorisationNothing is assessed without an explicit, recorded permission.
  3. AssessmentAn assessment cycle produces raw evidence, kept as evidence.
  4. FindingFindings are fingerprinted so a recurrence is recognised.
  5. Remediation and retestA remediation case, then an authorised retest.
  6. Closure or reopenClosed only on a passing retest; reopened if it recurs.
