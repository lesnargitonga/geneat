Theme probe

Every square below has an exact expected colour for the light and the dark theme. If a browser repaints this page on its own, the squares show it.

  1. Choose a theme with the switch at the top (System, Light or Dark).
  2. Take one screenshot per grid, with both coloured corner marks on screen.
  3. Send the screenshots with the browser's dark-mode setting noted. They are measured by tools/theme-matrix/analyse_screenshot.py.
