How we engineer

  1. Evidence before claimsSealed runs, measured results and stated limits. A system is described as far as its evidence goes.
  2. Secure by designFail-closed boundaries, audit trails, and offensive testing only against what we own or are authorised to test.
  3. We run what we buildDeployment, monitoring and support continue after launch, instead of a repository handed over.
