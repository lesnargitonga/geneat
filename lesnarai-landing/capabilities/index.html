What we
take on

Six kinds of work, each with a system you can inspect. Where the proof belongs to a client, we describe it without their name.

Payments and reconciliation

  1. The problemMoney that has to move through the instruments people actually use, and add up at the end of the day.
  2. What we buildOrdering, booking and payment flows on mobile money and WhatsApp, with merchant and staff back offices.
  3. What you getA working product, its reconciliation and its daybook, tested under contention.

Evidence: BizMtaani · CarePro · Gen-Eat demo

Trust and verification

  1. The problemDecisions where a wrong answer costs someone money or safety.
  2. What we buildEvidence models, verification gates, audit chains and authorised security assessment.
  3. What you getA system whose every decision can be traced and re-checked.

Evidence: Zygos · SentinelCore

Autonomy and simulation

  1. The problemMachines that must be proven before they are trusted in the field.
  2. What we buildControl bridges, mission logic, telemetry and sealed simulation runs.
  3. What you getA stack proven in simulation, with the failures it found and fixed.

Evidence: Operation Sentinel

Geospatial and logistics

  1. The problemSupply and demand that sit in different places and cannot see each other.
  2. What we buildMaps, matching and routing on real roads, from your data or open data.
  3. What you getA map-led product with the routing stored and licensed properly.

Evidence: MediMatch

Devices and television

  1. The problemLow-cost devices people already own, made to do more.
  2. What we buildNative Android TV software and local multiplayer, measured on the real hardware.
  3. What you getSoftware tuned to the device, with its measurements on record.

Evidence: LESNAR TV · LESNAR PLAY

AI with a person in the loop

  1. The problemAI near customers, money or code, where errors are expensive.
  2. What we buildTenant-bounded retrieval, pinned guardrails, model evaluation before adoption, and human approval.
  3. What you getAn AI system that fails closed and asks before it acts.

Evidence: Cypher · The Model foundry
