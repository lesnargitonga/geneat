Failures are found in the data

A simulated drone stood still for 8.8 minutes in front of a wall that was not there. The telemetry showed why: the lidar model turned a 1 m × 38 m wall into a 19 m disc. The fix was checked against brute-force ray marching on 3,000 random shapes before it flew again. The full case.

Gates that widen

A passing gate is evidence about the gate, not the page. Every real defect we found came from widening what was measured.

  1. StaticStructure, links, metadata and the content-security policy, without a browser.
  2. RuntimeEvery page in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit, at phone and desktop widths, with accessibility checks.
  3. BehaviourNo JavaScript, reduced motion and keyboard only; proven by failing on deliberately broken pages first.
  4. Transfer and performanceWhat each page actually downloads, and how fast it paints.
